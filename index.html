SEPT 18 / CLINICAL

SGD: Silence Inside the Vehicle

+

Highlights of My Learning

When oxygen availability is limited, oxidative phosphorylation is impaired. Glycolysis can continue to supply ATP, with lactate formation helping regenerate the NAD⁺ needed to keep it running.

How my understanding changed

There will be no fallback when one biochemistry progress is failing, but now learning this chapter led me to understanding that our body is able to have fallbacks when one is failing.

From Biochemistry to Medicine

Carbon monoxide binds hemoglobin with approximately 200-fold greater affinity than oxygen and shifts the oxygen dissociation curve to the left. This reduces oxygen delivery to tissues and helps explain the danger of tissue hypoxia in carbon monoxide poisoning.

The citric acid cycle

+

Highlights of My Learning

I appreciated how the TCA cycle acts as the metabolic grand central station, integrating the breakdown products of carbohydrates, fats, and proteins for complete oxidation.

Connecting the dots

Glycolysis and fatty acid oxidation feed into the cycle as Acetyl-CoA → driving the generation of reduced coenzymes (NADH and FADH2​) → which directly fuel the electron transport chain for ATP production, while simultaneously accepting carbon skeletons from amino acid catabolism.

How my understanding changed

The Citric Acid Cycle is just for carbs. Now it’s actually composed of so many processes such as taking pieces from fats, proteins, and sugars then breaking it down for energy for anabolism.

My Biochem Core Memory

The mnemonics that Doc A taught stayed in my mind.

My Muddiest Point

SEPT 25 / DISCUSSION

Glycogen metabolism, revisited

+

Highlights of My Learning

I understood how the reciprocal covalent and allosteric regulation of glycogenolysis and glycogenesis precisely buffers blood glucose during fasting and exercise.

How my understanding changed

Glycogen breakdown was just the exact reverse of its synthesis pathway but now I understand that glycogenolysis and glycogenesis utilize separate enzymatic routes under reciprocal hormonal control to prevent wasteful futile cycles.

From Biochemistry to Medicine

The concept of how the biochemical process of what’s happening whether you’re fed or fasting.

Connecting the dots

Fed-state glycogenesis storing glucose reserves → hormonal triggers (glucagon & epinephrine) activating glycogenolysis during fasting or exercise → release of glucose-1-phosphate → conversion to glucose-6-phosphate either for local energy via glycolysis (muscle) or dephosphorylation for systemic blood glucose maintenance (liver).

My Biochem Core Memory

Ninjanerd was such a big help because it felt like I was getting overwhelmed with so many things to study.

My Muddiest Point

The volume was a lot since the topic was almost at the end of midterms and the stress was creeping on me. I kept having a hard time memorizing and understanding the core of the metabolism of glycogen since it felt a lot.

08
MIDTERM REVIEW / REVIEW

Connecting the whole picture

+

Highlights of My Learning

All the chapters are connected to each other. Making it easier to understand yet difficult at the same time since they are a lot to understand through the core.

How my understanding changed

Metabolic pathways were isolated, linear checklists of chemical reactions happening independently in the cell. Now, I understand that they form a tightly integrated, reciprocal network where cellular energy charge, allosteric effectors, and hormonal signals continuously shift metabolic flux between storage, oxidation, and synthesis to survive changing physiological states.

From Biochemistry to Medicine

Connecting these molecular pathways clarifies clinical conditions: cyanide inhibits Complex IV and disrupts ATP production; carbon monoxide impairs oxygen delivery and can also affect cellular respiration; glycogen storage diseases, such as von Gierke disease, impair fasting glucose maintenance; and excessive hepatic gluconeogenesis contributes to hyperglycemia in uncontrolled diabetes mellitus.

My Biochem Core Memory

Realizing that the entire elaborate machinery of the citric acid cycle and the electron transport chain is essentially a molecular turbine designed to systematically strip electrons from simple sugar bonds and convert them into the electrochemical energy that powers human life.

My Muddiest Point

Keeping track of how opposing pathways like glycolysis and gluconeogenesis avoid running simultaneously.

01JUL 23DiscussionA less intimidating beginning.Introduction to Biochemistry · Water & pH+

During my first discussion in Biochemistry, I was initially nervous because of the stories and experiences I had heard and read about the subject. Since our first topic was the Introduction to Biochemistry, I expected to feel overwhelmed by the amount of information. However, the discussion was introduced in a light-hearted and engaging manner, which made the subject feel more approachable than I had anticipated.

Although there were concepts that were unfamiliar to me, I realized that understanding them became easier through reviewing them after the discussion. After each discussion, I made it a habit to reread my notes and skim my textbooks to strengthen my understanding. This experience taught me that Biochemistry may be challenging, but with consistent effort, curiosity, and willingness to review, difficult concepts can become more manageable.

Highlights of my learning

  • Biochemistry provides the chemical foundation of medicine, explaining how molecules interact and support life.
  • Water’s polarity and hydrogen bonding give it properties essential to biological processes.
  • pH, acids, bases, and buffers help maintain the environment necessary for biochemical reactions.

How my understanding changed

I thought Biochemistry was about memorizing complicated chemical concepts. Now, I understand that those concepts explain the body’s function, disease, physiology, and treatment.

Connecting the dots

Water → pH → hydrogen bonding → molecular interactions → biomolecule structure and function.

From Biochemistry to Medicine

Understanding pH, buffers, water, and molecular interactions provides a foundation for interpreting laboratory results, medications, and patient care.

06SEP 01ReviewSlowing down to understand the reaction.Prelims preparation · Enzymes & kinetics+

Highlights of my learning

  • Enzymes act as biological catalysts, allowing important reactions to occur more efficiently.
  • Michaelis–Menten kinetics, Km, and Vmax help describe enzyme behavior.
  • Different types of enzyme inhibition affect reactions in different ways.

How my understanding changed

Before, I knew enzymes made reactions faster. Now, I understand that they lower activation energy and that their activity can be influenced by conditions and inhibitors.

Connecting the dots

Protein structure → enzyme structure → active site → substrate binding → enzyme activity.

From Biochemistry to Medicine

Changes in enzyme activity can indicate disease or tissue damage. Many medications inhibit or modify enzyme activity, connecting kinetics with laboratory findings and pharmacology.

Core memory

I learned to approach these topics slowly instead of cramming, because there is a lot to understand.

My muddiest point

Class suspensions led to adjustments in lessons and quizzes. Learning enzymes remotely was challenging, but I tried my best to understand the concepts.
